Doing/Undoing is a kinetic installation constructed
using paper sculptures, wire, mirrors and hollow
glass objects. The pieces are performed using
light as a medium. 

The site specific pieces have been constructed and performed at:

The Indian Sonic Research Organisation
Artist-in-Residence, March 2025

Quadraphonic Disco, April 2025
Srishti Manipal Institute, performed with
sound artist Nikki Buzzi

LURU Mag at Sabha, December 2025
Performed with Vasu Dixit Collective

The ISRO Annual Day, December 2024
Performed with sound artist Yashas Shetty

Studio Show, Zurich, June 2024
Performed with sound artist Nikki Buzzi
